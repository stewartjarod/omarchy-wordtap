# Changelog

All notable changes to this plugin are listed here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the plugin follows
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). `manifest.json` holds the current version.

## [Unreleased]

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added
- other languages: foreign words and accented OCR

## [0.2.1] - 2026-10-19

### Fixed
- look up accented words and phrases

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added
- look up a highlighted phrase by tapping on it

## [0.1.2] - 2026-10-19

### Fixed
- show the word just tapped, not the previous one or nothing

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-19

### Fixed
- keep looked-up text off command lines

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-16

### Added
- three-finger tap word lookup with Wiktionary, Wikipedia and Webster's 1913

### Fixed
- pick the right word on scaled outputs and exit the watcher with the shell
- **ci:** keep the repo URL out of curl calls and ignore unresolved Quickshell types in qmllint
